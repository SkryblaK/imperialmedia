## 2. The legal position in Namibia

2.1 Article 13 of the Namibian Constitution protects the right to privacy. As at the date of this policy, Namibia has no comprehensive data protection statute in force and no dedicated data protection authority. A Data Protection Bill is making its way through the legislative process.

2.2 We nevertheless handle personal information according to widely accepted data protection principles: lawfulness, fairness and transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, security and accountability. Where it helps you, we describe your rights in terms similar to those used in modern data protection laws.

2.3 When Namibian data protection legislation takes effect, we will update this policy and our practices to comply with it. If you are in another country whose law applies to our handling of your information, that law applies in addition to this policy where it requires.
